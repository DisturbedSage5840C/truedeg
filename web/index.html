Inchident — reading tyre degradation off lap time

Within one stint, tyre age and fuel load move together almost perfectly, so a raw "lap time vs tyre age" line means nothing. Inchident pools 13 races, models lap time from tyre age plus context, then inverts for the one thing it actually wants: the degradation coefficient.

lap time = circuit baseline + d × tyre age + curvature × age² + fuel + track evolution + thermal  →  solve for d
